Four hours: how many bookings to break even?

Supplies: HK$35 per bookingFixed costs: HK$240 per shiftMy time: HK$50 per hour
Surplus after the cost of my timeHK$55
Four-hour schedule

Dark: service · tan: 15 min cleanup per booking · pale: unused time

Calculation steps · current inputsModel inputs · 4 h · HK$35 supplies/booking · HK$240 fixed/shift · time HK$50/h
  1. Time per slot60 + 15 = 75 min
  2. Capacity⌊240 ÷ 75⌋ = 3
  3. Completed bookingsmin(3, 3) = 3
  4. Revenue200 × 3 = HK$600
  5. Supplies35 × 3 = HK$105
  6. Cash surplus600 − 105 − 240 = HK$255
  7. Time value240 ÷ 60 × 50 = HK$200
  8. After time cost255 − 200 = HK$55
  9. Contribution per booking200 − 35 = HK$165
  10. Break-even incl. time⌈(240 + 200) ÷ 165⌉ = 3
  11. Minimum price at this volume35 + (240 + 200) ÷ 3 → HK$181.67
  12. Slots vs. break-even3 = 3 · no spare slot
⌊ ⌋ round down · ⌈ ⌉ round up · minimum price rounded up to the cent
